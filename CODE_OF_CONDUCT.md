# Code of Conduct

Natter follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussions about
the work. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not tolerated, in issues, pull
requests or anywhere else the project is represented.

Never post other people's conversations, names or screenshots of their messages when reporting a
problem — blur or crop them first.

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, report it privately through
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or by contacting the maintainer on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer
may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these rules.
